# Ontology Learning Policy

Ontology and KG learning produces proposals only.

Allowed proposal paths:

- `outputs/ontology_learning/term_mapping_proposals.jsonl`
- `outputs/ontology_learning/concept_proposals.jsonl`
- `outputs/ontology_learning/relation_proposals.jsonl`
- `outputs/ontology_learning/feature_mapping_proposals.jsonl`
- `outputs/ontology_learning/data_binding_proposals.jsonl`

No proposal may be promoted to `VERIFIED` without human approval and regression checks.
