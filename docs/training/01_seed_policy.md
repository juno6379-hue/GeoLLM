# Seed Policy

- Seeds are human-authored and carry `human_seed` plus `APPROVED` or `REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
- Existing benchmarks may inform taxonomy coverage but are protected holdouts and cannot be copied into training.
- `APPROVED` requires verified semantics, slots, registry references, tool/data dependencies, and execution behavior.
- A review-required authoring seed is inventory, not training material and cannot be expanded.
- One seed family stays in one split, including conversational continuations.
